Is Revelcy safe?

Yes! All SOL contributed is held securely in smart contracts. Funds cannot be accessed by the token creator. Refunds are automatic:

  • If the pre-market goal isn't met, everyone is refunded
  • If the creator doesn’t launch within 24 hours after the deadline, everyone is refunded
  • You can also leave at any time during pre-market and get an instant refund

How do the pre-markets work?

  1. Entry price follows the Pump.fun bonding curve (earlier = cheaper)
  2. Max 250 participants, 0.05–5 SOL each
  3. Pre-markets can run from 1 hour to 1 month
  4. Core token data, pre-market goal and vesting can't be changed. Deadline can be extended once (up to 2 weeks)
  5. You can exit pre-market at any time, but you’ll lose your place on the bonding curve
  6. If someone who joined before you leaves, you get better price (more tokens)
  7. Once the goal and deadline are met, the creator can launch on Pump.fun with one click
  8. Everyone can claim their tokens with one click

How does vesting work?

  1. Creators can choose to enable vesting for pre-market participants
  2. If enabled, tokens bought in pre-market will unlock partly at launch (0-75%), with the rest released gradually over 3 hours to 1 year
  3. Vesting schedule cannot be altered, and applies to everyone, including the creator
  4. You can always check vesting status on the token page

How do Revelcy tokens appear on Pump.fun and trading bots?

  • Revelcy tokens look and trade exactly like any Pump.fun token and are automatically indexed by trading bots, explorers, and aggregators
  • The wallet you used to create a token on Revelcy will be the Pump.fun deployer wallet
  • All info and links will appear as you set them during the creation. Description will also contain a disclaimer:

“The pre-market was done with revelcy.com. More: <premarket link>”

This builds trust and transparency for later buyers. We also recommend adding the premarket link to Dexscreener, your website, etc.
